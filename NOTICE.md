# Third-party datasets and papers

This project builds on publicly available fairness / bias NLP resources.
When redistributing **processed** datasets or reporting results, cite the originals:

- **StereoSet** — Nadeem et al., arXiv:2004.09456 — https://stereoset.mit.edu
- **CrowS-Pairs** — Nangia et al.
- **WinoBias** — Zhao et al. (`uclanlp/wino_bias`)
- **ToxiGen** — Hartvigsen et al. (`toxigen/toxigen-data`)
- **SQuAD** — Rajpurkar et al.
- **Tatoeba** — Tatoeba project
- **Encoder backbone** — `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`

Dataset content may include stereotypical or toxic language used for research on bias mitigation.
Use only for lawful research and evaluation.
